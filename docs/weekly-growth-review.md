# Weekly growth review

Use this review to decide whether the next public change should deepen a proven guide or introduce one fully supported vehicle cluster. Do not publish a new vehicle simply because it is next in the backlog.

## Monday sequence

1. Read the 08:30 local weekly quality audit. Resolve broken links, unsupported source claims, stale prices, missing images, sitemap mistakes, or build failures before planning new content.
2. Read the 09:15 local content queue review. A queued vehicle is a candidate, not a publication instruction. Generate a draft only when the required evidence, images, fitment notes, and product research are complete.
3. Review GA4 and Search Console manually. The local automations do not have access to those accounts. Look at search queries and landing pages, engaged sessions, scroll depth, ownership-task clicks, search-result selections, and no-result searches.
4. Choose one focused outcome: strengthen an existing core guide that has demand or a clear abandonment point, or publish one new model only when its full cluster meets the readiness gate.
5. Make the change, run the build and repository health check, and add an Updates entry only after the public release is complete.

## Content decision rules

- Prioritize the RAV4 and Civic when readers search for a missing step, leave after a guide, or cannot find a relevant result.
- Add direct claim-level sources beside reliability, maintenance, recall, and specification statements. Do not treat internal notes as a public source.
- Publish parts content only with exact fitment scope, a dated price check, a product-level price source, alternatives, and a clear disclosure. It stays `noindex` until it earns an indexing review.
- Use original field notes, photographs, or test results only when they genuinely exist and can be described accurately. Do not create implied testing evidence.
- Keep each update narrow enough to verify: a checklist, a diagnosis flow, a maintenance record, or a comparison with cited differences is preferable to a broad template expansion.

## Monthly review

Once per month, compare indexed pages with traffic and Search Console coverage. Improve or consolidate pages that lack a distinct use case, and leave archive, product, brand, model, category, and generic symptom pages out of the sitemap unless their content has become independently useful.
