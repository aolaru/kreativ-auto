# Original evidence intake

Only publish first-hand evidence that genuinely exists and can be independently described. This record is for photos, viewing notes, workshop invoices, measurements, and test observations; it is not a place to recreate information from memory or online sources.

## Required record

- Date and location at the level needed to understand conditions. Do not publish a precise private address.
- Vehicle identity: year, model, trim, drivetrain, mileage, VIN only when disclosure is appropriate, and relevant modifications.
- Source material: original photo filename, invoice, report, measurement, or contemporaneous note.
- Observation: what was seen, heard, measured, or documented; distinguish it from an inference or diagnosis.
- Conditions: cold or warm, road type, speed range, weather, load, warning lights, tools used, and repeatability where relevant.
- Permission and privacy: confirm the photographer or document owner permits publication and remove personal details before publishing.

## Editorial use

- A photo can document appearance or a visible condition only. It does not prove the cause of a noise, a product fitment, or a common fault.
- An invoice documents stated work and date. It does not by itself confirm that a symptom was resolved unless a later observation says so.
- A personal road-test note describes one vehicle in stated conditions. It must not be presented as a fleet result, failure rate, or universal model finding.
- Link an original item beside the claim it supports, state its limits, and retain the unedited source outside the public site when appropriate.
- Do not add credentials, ownership, testing, workshop access, or results that are not documented in the record.

## Publication decision

Use original evidence only after the author verifies the record, removes private information, and adds the source date and scope to the relevant guide. When that evidence is absent, use a cited manufacturer, regulator, or product source instead and state the limit clearly.
