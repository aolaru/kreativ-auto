# Code Of Conduct

## Expected conduct

Project participants are expected to communicate respectfully, discuss technical and
editorial claims using evidence, accept correction, and keep feedback focused on the
work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate misinformation, and disclosure
of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review
comments, and other spaces used to represent the project.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to `info@kreativauto.com`. Include links or other
context needed to review the incident. The project owner may edit, hide, reject, or
remove contributions and participation that violate this policy.
