# Image And Brand Asset Licensing

The repository's MIT and editorial-content licenses do not automatically apply to
files under `public/images/`.

## Kreativ Auto branding

The Kreativ Auto name, logo, favicon, and visual brand identity are reserved by the
project owner. No trademark or brand-use license is granted. Forks should replace
these assets and use a name that does not imply an official Kreativ Auto deployment.

This restriction includes:

- `public/favicon.svg`
- `public/images/logo-mark-alt.svg`
- other files whose primary purpose is to identify Kreativ Auto

## Credited third-party photographs

The vehicle photographs listed in `src/data/image-credits.ts` retain their stated
licenses. At the time this notice was prepared, the listed photographs were sourced
from Wikimedia Commons under CC BY-SA 4.0. Their resized and WebP derivatives must
retain attribution and share-alike terms.

The source page, author, license URL, and modification note for each credited file
are authoritative in `src/data/image-credits.ts` and on the website's Image Credits
page.

## Other photographs and product imagery

No reuse license is granted by this repository for an image that is not explicitly
listed in `src/data/image-credits.ts`. This includes unlisted vehicle photographs,
product photographs, and their thumbnails under:

- `public/images/photos/`
- `public/images/thumbs/`

Those files are retained so the existing website and its history remain intact, but
they are excluded from the open-source and editorial-content licenses. Before
redistributing or using one in a fork, obtain permission from its rights holder or
replace it with an asset whose provenance and license you can document.

## Contributions

Every new third-party image must include its source URL, creator, license, license
URL, local file path, and a description of modifications. Do not submit retailer,
manufacturer, stock-library, or web-search images unless their terms expressly allow
the proposed repository and website use.
