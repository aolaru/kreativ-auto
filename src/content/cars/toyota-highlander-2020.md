Before a purchase, compare the service record with Toyota's 2020 warranty and maintenance guide, then check the VIN for open recalls. The published recall record helps establish campaign context; it does not show whether a particular vehicle has completed the repair.

On a road test, separate ordinary brake noise from grinding, vibration, pull, warning lights, or changed pedal feel. Check tire condition and wheel balance before attaching highway vibration to a larger suspension or bearing repair.
