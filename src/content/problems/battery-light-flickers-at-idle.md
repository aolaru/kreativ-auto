  - Battery or charging light flickers at idle and disappears once revs rise
  - Interior lights dim slightly at stoplights
  - The car still starts, but voltage-related behavior seems less stable than normal
causes:
  - Aging battery that is no longer buffering voltage well
  - Weak charging output that is most obvious at low engine speed
  - Ground or connection resistance causing unstable system voltage
solutions:
  - Test battery health and charging voltage together instead of separately
  - Inspect battery terminals and ground connections before ordering major parts
  - Confirm whether the issue is isolated to idle or shows up under load too
