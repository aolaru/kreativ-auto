symptoms:
  - Idle speed fluctuates or the engine feels uneven at a stop
  - Check engine light flashes or stores a misfire code
  - Rough cold start improves slightly once the engine warms up
causes:
  - Worn spark plugs or weak ignition coils causing incomplete combustion
  - Vacuum leaks introducing unmetered air at low RPM
  - Dirty injectors or fuel delivery issues affecting one cylinder more than the others
solutions:
  - Scan for misfire codes and inspect spark plugs before replacing multiple parts at once
  - Test ignition coils and swap suspect coils between cylinders if the platform allows it
  - Check intake hoses and vacuum lines for leaks before moving into deeper fuel diagnostics
