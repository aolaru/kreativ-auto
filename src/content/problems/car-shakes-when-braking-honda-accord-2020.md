Brake shake is one of the easiest Accord complaints to overcomplicate. The useful first question is whether the vibration appears only under brake pressure or is already present while cruising.

Once the pattern is confirmed, the repair path can stay narrow: rotor surface, pad condition, hardware service, and wheel torque before broader suspension theories.
