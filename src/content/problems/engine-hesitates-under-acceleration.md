symptoms:
  - The engine pauses or feels flat when you press the throttle
  - The hesitation is worse when merging, climbing, or accelerating from low speed
  - Throttle response feels inconsistent even though the engine still idles acceptably
causes:
  - Weak ignition coils or worn spark plugs that break down more under load
  - Dirty airflow or intake issues that disrupt the air-fuel balance during throttle changes
  - Fuel delivery problems such as weak pressure or injector performance issues
solutions:
  - Scan for codes and misfire counts before replacing sensors or fuel parts
  - Inspect ignition components first if maintenance is overdue or the hesitation shows up under load
  - Check intake connections and airflow-related items before moving deeper into fuel diagnosis

  - Jumping straight to fuel parts before checking plugs and coils

  - Testing only idle behavior even though the real issue shows up under load

faqs:
  - question: Can bad ignition coils cause hesitation under acceleration?
    answer: Yes. Weak coils often show up more clearly under load than at idle because the engine needs a stronger, more consistent spark.

When an engine hesitates under acceleration, the expensive mistake is assuming the problem must be something complicated. A lot of the time the answer is still basic ignition or airflow maintenance that was easy to miss until the engine was asked to work harder.
