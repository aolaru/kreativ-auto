Low-speed brake squeal makes the Corolla feel worse than it is. Most of the time, the job is still about pad choice, glazing, or hardware service rather than some bigger brake-system failure.

The key is not to treat every squeal like a full brake disaster. Confirm pad condition, rotor surface, and the contact points first, then decide whether the car needs better pads or a more complete brake refresh.
