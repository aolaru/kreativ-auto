symptoms:
  - A dull clunk or knock from the front suspension on broken pavement
  - Noise is easier to hear at low speed than on smooth highway roads
  - The front end still drives mostly fine, but sounds loose over bumps
causes:
  - Worn sway bar links or bushings allowing small suspension movement to knock
  - Strut mounts or control-arm bushings starting to loosen up
  - Loose brake or suspension hardware after previous service
solutions:
  - Check sway bar links and visible front-end hardware first because they are common and easy to miss
  - Inspect bushings, mounts, and fasteners before assuming the struts are bad
  - Recheck torque and surrounding hardware if the noise started after suspension or brake work
urgency: Low to medium unless handling has changed or the noise is getting worse quickly
canYouDrive: Usually yes for short-term driving, but persistent clunking should be inspected before extra wear builds up

diyDifficulty: Easy to moderate for inspection, moderate for replacing front suspension wear items
whenToSeeMechanic:
  - The clunk is getting louder or is joined by loose steering feel
  - You can see torn bushings, leaking struts, or obviously worn hardware
  - The noise started right after other front-end work and you cannot isolate it
commonMistakes:
  - Replacing struts first when the real issue is a small link or bushing
  - Ignoring torque checks after recent brake or suspension service
