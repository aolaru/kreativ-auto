symptoms:
  - Rear brakes squeak most on the first few stops after rain or washing the SUV
  - Noise often fades once the brakes warm up, then returns in damp conditions
  - Braking still feels normal, but the rear of the SUV sounds cheap or gritty at low speed
causes:
  - Pad compound that is more prone to moisture-related surface noise
  - Rear brake hardware or contact points that are dry, dirty, or not moving cleanly
  - Light rear rotor surface rust that clears slowly with the existing pad setup
solutions:
  - Inspect rear pad wear, hardware condition, and rotor surface before replacing parts blindly
  - Clean and lubricate the proper rear brake contact points during service
  - Switch to a quieter rear pad compound if the noise keeps returning with otherwise healthy brakes
urgency: Low unless the squeak is joined by grinding, vibration, or uneven rear braking

whenToSeeMechanic:
  - The rear squeak turns into grinding or rough braking feel
  - Pad wear is uneven or rotor scoring is visible
  - The noise keeps returning right after rear brake service
commonMistakes:
  - Replacing front pads when the noise is clearly from the rear

  - Assuming every wet-weather squeak means the rotors are ruined
quickVerdict: A damp-weather rear squeak is usually a pad-and-hardware refinement problem before it is a major brake failure.

  - Owners often mistake a moisture-related squeak for ruined rotors when the hardware or pad compound is still the more likely answer.

faqs:
  - question: Is rear brake squeak after rain always a bad sign?
    answer: Not always. Light moisture-related brake noise can be normal, but repeated squeak still points to a pad or hardware setup worth checking.
  - question: Why does the noise fade after a few stops?
    answer: The first brake applications clear moisture and light rotor rust, which is why the sound often fades once the brakes warm up.

Rear brake squeak after rain is one of those RAV4 complaints that sounds worse than it usually is. The useful move is to treat it like a brake-setup problem first, not like a mystery failure.
