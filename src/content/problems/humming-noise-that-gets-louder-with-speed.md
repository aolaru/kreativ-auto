symptoms:
  - Low humming or growling that increases with road speed
  - Noise changes slightly when steering weight shifts left or right
  - Vibration or roughness felt through the floor at highway speed
causes:
  - Worn wheel bearing or hub assembly
  - Tire cupping or uneven tread wear
  - Driveline or differential noise in more advanced cases
solutions:
  - Rotate tires and road-test again before replacing a hub blindly
  - Inspect hub play and listen for bearing roughness with the wheel off the ground
  - Replace the noisy hub assembly and verify alignment or tire condition afterward
