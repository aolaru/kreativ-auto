Light low-speed squeal with normal braking can start with pad material, rotor surface, and hardware condition. It is not enough to identify the pad brand; the inspection must also establish pad thickness, rotor condition, and the exact axle and trim.

Grinding, vibration, pull, warning lights, a soft pedal, or a longer stopping distance changes the situation. Stop treating it as a noise concern and arrange a brake-system inspection before ordering parts.
