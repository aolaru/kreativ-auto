The Mazda 3 is quiet enough that brake squeal stands out quickly. That does not automatically make it a major brake failure, but it does mean the inspection should be specific.

Start with pad condition, hardware service, and rotor surface. If those point to replacement, choose pads for quiet daily use instead of chasing an aggressive setup the car does not need.
