symptoms:
  - The engine stumbles or shakes for the first few seconds after startup
  - Idle improves noticeably once the engine begins to warm up
  - Cold starts feel weaker or slower than usual on cool mornings
causes:
  - Worn spark plugs or weak ignition coils that struggle most on cold startup
  - Small vacuum leaks that matter more before the engine settles into normal idle
  - Fuel delivery or injector issues that show up most clearly when the engine is cold
solutions:
  - Scan for pending misfire codes and inspect the spark plugs before buying multiple parts
  - Check coils, intake hoses, and vacuum lines before assuming the issue is fuel-related
  - If maintenance is overdue, handle plugs and ignition parts first before chasing less common causes
urgency: Low to medium if the roughness clears quickly and no warning light is flashing

  - The check engine light flashes during or after startup

faqs:
  - question: Why does my car only start rough when cold?
    answer: Cold starts put more stress on weak plugs, coils, air leaks, and fuel delivery problems, so small issues often show up there first.
  - question: Can bad spark plugs cause a rough cold start?
    answer: Yes. Worn plugs are one of the most common reasons an otherwise healthy engine stumbles briefly right after startup.

A rough cold start is usually one of those problems that gives you an early warning before the car starts misfiring all the time. That is why it makes sense to check the basic ignition and air-leak items early instead of waiting for the symptom to get louder.
