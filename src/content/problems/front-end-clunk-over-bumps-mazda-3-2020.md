A front-end clunk on the Mazda 3 usually sounds worse than the repair really is at first. That is why the best move is to catch the small hardware wear before multiple front-end parts start sounding tired together.

Owners lose time when they treat every clunk like a whole-suspension mystery. Start narrow, confirm the play, and only expand the job if the inspection makes that necessary.
