symptoms:
  - The front end knocks or clunks when one wheel loads up entering driveways or angled ramps
  - The SUV feels mostly normal on smooth roads, but small body movement creates a distinct front knock
  - The sound is easiest to reproduce at low speed rather than at highway pace
causes:
  - Worn front sway bar links or bushings allowing suspension movement to knock
  - Loose front-end hardware after prior brake or suspension work
  - Strut-mount or bushing wear that shows up most clearly when the front suspension articulates unevenly
solutions:
  - Check front sway bar links and visible hardware first because they are common and relatively easy to confirm
  - Inspect mounts and bushings before assuming the full strut assembly is bad
  - Recheck recent front-end work if the noise started soon after another repair
urgency: Low to medium unless the clunk is getting louder or steering feel has changed

faqs:
  - question: Why is the clunk louder when entering a driveway at an angle?
    answer: That kind of entry loads the suspension unevenly, which makes link, bushing, and mount play easier to hear.

Driveway-entry clunks are one of the easiest RAV4 noises to overcall. The right move is to check the smaller front-end parts first, because that is where the repair often actually lives.
