Short-trip battery complaints usually start quietly: one slow crank, then a jump start, then another weak restart after errands. The Tucson is not unusual here, but the diagnosis still needs discipline.

Confirm the battery, charging behavior, and actual driving pattern before buying a replacement. That keeps a simple battery job from becoming a vague electrical parts hunt.
