symptoms:
  - Rear brakes squeak on the first few stops after the car sat overnight
  - The noise is worse after rain, humidity, or a car wash
  - Braking feel still seems normal even though the sound is annoying
causes:
  - Light surface rust on the rotors after moisture exposure
  - Pad compound that is noisier in damp conditions
  - Brake hardware that is dirty, dry, or not controlling pad movement well
solutions:
  - Inspect rear brake hardware and pad condition before assuming the rear brakes need a full overhaul
  - Clean and lubricate the hardware correctly if the setup is still in good shape
  - Switch to a quieter pad compound if the current rear pads keep squeaking in normal use
