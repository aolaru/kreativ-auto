symptoms:
  - Repetitive clicking from the front end during slow turns
  - Noise is louder while accelerating through a tight corner
  - Split CV boot or grease around the inner wheel area
causes:
  - Outer CV joint wear after grease loss from a torn boot
  - Low-quality aftermarket axle with poor joint tolerances
  - Excessive suspension movement making an existing axle issue more noticeable
solutions:
  - Inspect CV boots and axle joints before assuming the noise is a steering rack problem
  - Replace the affected axle assembly if the joint clicks consistently under load
  - Recheck alignment and suspension hardware after axle replacement
